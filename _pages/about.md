I am an M.S. student in the Department of Artificial Intelligence Semiconductor at Hanyang University, advised by
Prof. Haejun Chung. My research focuses on the inverse design of metasurfaces and nanophotonic devices, combining
full-wave electromagnetic simulation with adjoint-based and machine-learning optimization. I am particularly interested
in how design methods scale from individual meta-atoms to full metasurface apertures, and I apply these methods to
metalenses, on-chip wavelength demultiplexers, and microwave-transparent metamaterials.

I received my B.S. in Applied Physics from Kyung Hee University in 2026, graduating summa cum laude and ranked first in
my department. There I worked with Prof. Sun-Kyung Kim on the Bayesian optimization of microwave-transparent metallic
metamaterials.

**Research interests**

- Inverse design of metasurfaces and nanophotonic devices
- Adjoint and topology optimization at large scale
- Machine learning for electromagnetic design
- Large-scale full-wave simulation (FDTD, MPI, GPU)
