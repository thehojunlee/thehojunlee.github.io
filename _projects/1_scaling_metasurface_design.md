**Problem.** Large-area metasurfaces are usually assembled from unit cells characterized under the local periodic
approximation, which neglects the electromagnetic coupling between dissimilar neighboring elements.

**Approach.** Our review classifies metasurface design methods by the spatial extent treated jointly in the
electromagnetic model — reusable unit cells, supercells and enlarged domains, and full-metasurface inverse design — and
discusses when each scale is appropriate.

**My contribution.** As a co-first author, I wrote the sections on supercell metasurfaces and neural surrogate solvers,
and performed full-wave FDTD benchmarks that compare unit-cell, supercell, and full-metasurface inverse design of the
same metalens.
