- **Dual-band metalenses.** Inverse design of a single metalens that focuses two wavelength bands, based on
  effective-medium theory.
- **Ultra-compact wavelength demultiplexers.** Adjoint-based inverse design of on-chip photonic demultiplexers with a
  small footprint.
- **Radar-transparent heater metasurfaces.** Coupled electromagnetic and thermal design of metallic patterns that heat
  a glass cover while transmitting W-band automotive radar.
