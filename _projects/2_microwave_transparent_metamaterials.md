**Problem.** Metallic films that conduct electricity or block electromagnetic interference also block microwave and
radar signals.

**Approach.** I developed a Bayesian optimization loop that couples CST full-wave simulation with MATLAB to find
metallic unit-cell patterns with a prescribed transmission band and metal fill fraction.
